love comes to me in different ways. i love love. i love music. i love people.i wanted this entry to be dedicated to the people i love. whether they're still present in my life or not, everyone has had an impact on me and shaped the way i am. my family is a part of my life especially my brother. we moved to the city together about a year ago and he's helped shape me into the person i am today. i look up to him in many ways.
i love my friends. coming to the city, i worried about making friends. i was worried that i would not find anyone that related to me. that liked the same things as me. turns out i did. i found a lot of people just like me actually. some stayed, some didn't. that's life i guess. 







to be honest, love also drives me crazy. this might just be but i crave love. i crave the intimacy of love. sharing my life with someone else. this profound feeling towards love is weird. a blessing and a curse really. hopefully one day i can find someone that feels the same obession with love or whoknows, maybe i already have.
